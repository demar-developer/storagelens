      How it works

      From a blur of files to a clear decision.

      1. 01

        Scan

        One pass over what Windows already knows about your files: names, sizes, dates and OneDrive states. No file is opened, and files OneDrive keeps online are never downloaded.

        1.47 million items in 18 seconds on our own PC

      2. 02

        Map

        Follow the space. Every folder is a block sized by what it really takes on disk. Click one to go inside, as deep as you like.

      3. 03

        Understand

        For well-known Windows, developer and app folders, StorageLens says what the folder is, the supported way to free its space and what not to do. Everything else gets an importance level and the reason for it.

      4. 04

        Decide

        A plan before any action. Mark what to keep and what to review, with your own backup notes. Saving a decision changes StorageLens' notes and nothing on your disk.

      The Assistant

      Ask in your own words. Watch the screen answer.

      The Assistant doesn't hand you a paragraph to trust. It opens the right view, sets the filters and puts a page together, and every number on that page comes from your scan, never from the AI.

      Try one

      Redrawn for the page on the same made-up library as the walkthrough, and the replies here are scripted. In the app a model on your PC picks the page and StorageLens builds it.

      • The model picks. StorageLens counts.

        A small model on your PC chooses which page answers you. The page is StorageLens' own, filled from the scan, and each answer says so: "check the filters it chose. The numbers come from your scan."

      • Other agents ask first.

        Claude Desktop, Hermes and other agents can connect over MCP and read your scan. When one wants to move your screen, you get a card: Show me, Not now, or Always allow.

      • No AI ever acts on a file.

        An agent can open views, highlight rows and build pages. It cannot delete, move, rename or read a file, start a scan, export, or change a setting. Those stay yours.

      What leaves this PC

      Nothing.

      No model runs and no agent can connect. The Assistant still answers its fixed questions from the scan, and "What is this?" still works, because the folder notes are not AI.

      • Names, not contents

        StorageLens measures from file-system information: names, sizes, dates and OneDrive states. It never opens a file to do it.

      • Off by default

        One switch, AI and agents, off until you turn it on. While it is off nothing is sent anywhere.

      • Your key stays yours

        A cloud model is your choice, with your own key, kept in Windows Credential Manager and sent only to the service it is for.

      • You see who is driving

        Whatever is on screen says who put it there, you, the Assistant or a named agent, and offers the way back.

      What happens next

      1. Windows will ask you to confirm. The files are not code-signed yet, so on a downloaded copy Windows shows “Windows protected your PC”: choose Run anyway; if you don't see it, choose More info first. Your browser may also say the file is not commonly downloaded: choose to keep it. Windows asks this of any new unsigned program; it says nothing about what the program does. Where Smart App Control is on, expect StorageLens to be blocked: it stops apps that are not signed and that Microsoft does not know yet, and it offers no Run anyway. Windows Security > App & browser control shows whether it is on; Windows has no setting to allow a single app there.
      2. Setup wears the look you chose. It shows where the app and your scans will go, and installs for your Windows account only.
      3. Press Start first scan. A new install starts empty. A scan reads names, sizes, dates and OneDrive states, never what is in a file.
      4. Later. To update, download the newer file and open it: your scans and notes stay, and the app does not check for updates itself. To remove it, use Windows Settings > Apps > Installed apps; your scans and notes are kept until you delete that folder yourself.
